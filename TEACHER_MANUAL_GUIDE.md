## 1. Logging In

Before you can use the portal, you need to log in with your teacher account.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari recommended).
   - For the best experience, we recommend using **Google Chrome** on a desktop or laptop computer.

2. **Go to the KNHS School Portal website.** Ask your school administrator for the correct URL if you don't know it.

   (insert screenshot — browser address bar showing the KNHS portal URL)

3. **You will see the login page** with fields for your email and password. The page will display the KNHS logo and the school name at the top.

   (insert screenshot — the login page with email and password fields visible)

4. **Enter your email address** in the "Email" field. This is the email your school administrator used when creating your account.

5. **Enter your password** in the "Password" field. You can click the eye icon (👁) next to the password field to reveal what you're typing and make sure it's correct.

6. **Click the "Sign In" button.** You will be automatically redirected to your Dashboard.

   (insert screenshot — the login page with credentials filled in and the Sign In button highlighted)

### What If You Can't Log In?

- **Forgot your password?** Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. You'll be asked to enter your email address, and a password reset link will be sent to your inbox. Follow the link to create a new password.
- **Account not working?** Contact the school IT administrator to verify your account is active and that you're using the correct email address.
- **Wrong role?** Make sure you're logging in with your teacher account, not a student or parent account. Each role has a different set of features.
- **Page shows an error?** Try refreshing the page. If the error persists, clear your browser cache (press `Ctrl + Shift + Delete` on Windows or `Cmd + Shift + Delete` on Mac) and try again.

> **Note:** Your account role is set to **Faculty/Staff**. This means you'll see teacher-specific pages in the sidebar, and you won't have access to admin-only features like user management or system settings.

## 3. My Classes

**Route:** `/my-classes`

"My Classes" is where you manage everything related to the classes you teach. Think of it as your personal teaching workspace.

### Viewing Your Classes

1. Click **"My Classes"** in the sidebar under the "Workspaces" section.

2. You'll see a grid of **classroom cards**, one for each class assigned to you. The cards are displayed in a responsive grid layout — on wider screens, you'll see 3 cards per row; on narrower screens, they stack vertically.

   (insert screenshot — the My Classes page showing a grid of classroom cards)

3. Each card shows the following information:
   - **Classroom name and grade level** — e.g., "Grade 7 - Rizal"
   - **Number of subjects** you teach in that class — displayed as a small badge
   - **Number of enrolled students** currently in the class — displayed as a number with a person icon

   (insert screenshot — a single classroom card with the name, subject count, and student count highlighted)

### Opening a Classroom

Click on any classroom card to open it. Inside each classroom, you'll find **7 tabs** that cover different aspects of class management:

| Tab | Purpose | What You Can Do |
|-----|---------|-----------------|
| **Stream** | Class announcements and feed | View and post class-specific announcements, see the activity feed |
| **Materials** | Learning materials | Upload, organize, and manage files like lesson plans, worksheets, and modules |
| **People** | Student roster | View the list of enrolled students, their statuses, and contact info |
| **Attendance** | Daily attendance | Mark attendance for each class period |
| **History** | Past records | Review attendance records from previous dates |
| **Grades** | Grade management | Input, edit, and manage student grades for each subject |
| **Analytics** | Performance insights | View charts and summaries of class performance trends |

(insert screenshot — the classroom page showing all 7 tabs at the top)

> **Tip:** The tabs are always in the same order, so you can quickly find what you need once you're familiar with the layout. The **Stream** tab is shown by default when you first open a classroom.

- **Post announcements** — Type a message in the text area at the top, optionally attach files, and click "Post" to share with your students.

## 4. Taking Attendance

Attendance tracking is one of your most important daily tasks. The portal supports two types of attendance: **schedule-based** (for subject teachers) and **class-level/homeroom** (for advisory teachers).

### Three Ways to Access Attendance

You can reach the attendance system from multiple entry points:

1. **Attendance Dashboard** (`/attendance-dashboard`) — Shows an overview of all your periods for any day, with completion status indicators.
2. **My Classes** → Select a class → **Attendance** tab — Shows your scheduled periods for that specific class.
3. **Direct link from the Dashboard** — Click any period card on the dashboard to go straight to attendance entry.

(insert screenshot — the three attendance entry points highlighted in the sidebar and Dashboard)

### Step-by-Step: Taking Schedule-Based Attendance

This is the most common workflow for subject teachers who have a set schedule.

#### Step 1: Open the Attendance Dashboard

Click **"Attendance Dashboard"** in the sidebar under the "Teaching" section.

(insert screenshot — the Attendance Dashboard link highlighted in the sidebar)

#### Step 2: Navigate to the Right Date

Use the navigation controls at the top of the calendar to select the correct date:

- **Arrow buttons (◀ ▶)** — Click the left arrow to go to the previous day, or the right arrow to go to the next day.
- **Date picker** — Click the date display to open a calendar popup. Select any date to jump directly to it.
- **"Today" button** — Click this to quickly return to the current date.

(insert screenshot — the date navigation bar with arrows, date picker, and Today button)

#### Step 3: Review Your Scheduled Periods

Each card on the dashboard represents one class period for the selected date. Each card displays:

- The **subject name** and **classroom** — e.g., "Mathematics - Grade 7-Rizal"
- The **time period** — e.g., "8:00 AM – 9:00 AM"
- The **student count** — e.g., "42 students"
- A **completion status** badge:
   - **"Completed"** (green badge) — Attendance has been submitted for this period
   - **"Pending"** (amber badge) — Attendance has not yet been taken
   - **"Draft"** (blue badge) — Attendance was saved as a draft but not yet submitted

(insert screenshot — the Attendance Dashboard showing multiple period cards with different status badges)

#### Step 4: Click a Period Card

Click on the period card you want to take attendance for. This opens the attendance entry screen for that specific subject and time slot.

(insert screenshot — the attendance entry screen showing the student list and status buttons)

#### Step 5: Mark Attendance for Each Student

For each student in the list, click one of the four status buttons next to their name:

| Status Button | Color | Icon | When to Use |
|---------------|-------|------|-------------|
| **Present** | Green | ✓ Checkmark | Student is present in class |
| **Absent** | Red | ✗ X mark | Student is absent from class |
| **Late** | Amber/Orange | 🕐 Clock | Student arrived after the start time |
| **Excused** | Blue | 🛡 Shield | Student is excused (e.g., medical leave, school activity) |

- The currently selected status button will appear **highlighted/raised** for each student.
- Optionally, add **Remarks** in the text field below each student's name. This is useful for noting reasons like "Sick leave," "Family emergency," or "Representing school in competition."

(insert screenshot — a student row showing the four status buttons and the remarks field)

#### Step 6: Use Bulk Actions to Speed Up the Process

Instead of clicking each student one by one, use the bulk action buttons:

- **"All Present"** button — Click this to mark every student in the class as Present at once. This is the fastest way to start: mark everyone as present first, then just change the exceptions (absent, late, excused).
- **"Clear"** button — Click this to reset all attendance marks back to the default state. Use this if you made mistakes and want to start over.

(insert screenshot — the "All Present" and "Clear" bulk action buttons at the top of the attendance form)

#### Step 7: Save or Submit

When you're done marking attendance, you have two options:

- **"Save" button** — Saves your attendance as a **Draft**. You can come back later to continue editing or make changes before submitting. Use this if you're interrupted or want to double-check your entries.
- **"Submit" button** — Finalizes and sends the attendance to the admin for review. Once submitted, you can no longer edit it directly (but you can click "Reopen" to move it back to Draft if needed).

> **Important:** Always double-check your attendance before clicking "Submit." Once submitted, the record is sent to the admin and changes require admin approval.

(insert screenshot — the Save and Submit buttons at the bottom of the attendance form)

### Time-Window Access Rules

The portal enforces time-window rules for attendance:

- **Earliest access:** Teachers can mark attendance starting **15 minutes before** the scheduled period begins.
- **Latest access:** Attendance can be marked until **5:00 PM** (end of the school day).
- **Admin override:** Admins can mark attendance at any time without these restrictions.

If you try to access attendance outside these windows, you'll see a message explaining the time restriction.

### Dual Attendance Types

| Type | When Used | Description |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Schedule-Based** | Subject teachers (e.g., Math, English, Science teachers) | Marks attendance for a specific subject period that is linked to your daily schedule. You'll see the subject name and time on the attendance card. |
| **Class-Level (Homeroom)** | Advisory teachers (homeroom advisers) | Marks attendance for the entire class at once, without being tied to a specific schedule. Used during homeroom periods. The attendance covers all students in the class regardless of their individual schedules. |

### Attendance Status Workflow

Attendance goes through a simple workflow once submitted:

```
Draft → Submitted → (Admin reviews) → Approved / Locked
```

| Status | What It Means | What You Can Do |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Draft** | Your attendance is still being edited | Continue marking or editing student statuses. Click "Save" to keep your progress. |
| **Submitted** | Sent to admin for review | You can click **"Reopen"** to move it back to Draft if you need to make changes. |
| **Approved** | Admin has approved the attendance | The record is finalized and visible to students and parents. No further edits are possible. |
| **Locked** | Admin has locked the attendance record | This is the final state. No changes are possible under any circumstances. Contact the admin if corrections are urgently needed. |

### Viewing Attendance History

To review past attendance records:

1. Open a classroom from **My Classes**.
2. Click the **History** tab.
3. You'll see a list of past attendance records organized by date. Click any date to expand and see the period-by-period details.

   (insert screenshot — the History tab showing a list of dates with attendance records)

4. Each expanded date shows:
   - The **period/subject** for each record
   - The **status** (Present, Absent, Late, Excused) for each student
   - The **total count** of present, absent, late, and excused students

   (insert screenshot — an expanded date showing period-by-period attendance details)

## 5. Grading

### Accessing the Grade Dashboard

There are two ways to access the Grade Dashboard:

1. **From the sidebar:** Click **"Grade Submission"** in the sidebar under the "Teaching" section.
2. **Direct URL:** Navigate to `/teacher-grade-dashboard` in your browser's address bar.

(insert screenshot — the Grade Submission link in the sidebar)

When the Grade Dashboard loads, you'll see:

- **Active grading period info** with a countdown timer showing how much time remains before the grading period closes
- **Cards for each subject/classroom** you teach, showing grading progress (e.g., "3 of 25 grades entered")
- **Filter buttons** at the top: Pending, Submitted, Overdue, Approved/Locked, All — click any filter to narrow the view

(insert screenshot — the Grade Dashboard showing subject cards, the grading period countdown, and filter buttons)

### Step-by-Step: Inputting Grades

#### Step 1: Select a Subject/Classroom

From the Grade Dashboard, click **"Enter Grades"** on the card for the subject/classroom you want to grade.

- This opens the **Grades** tab inside My Classes for that specific classroom.
- You'll see a list of students enrolled in that class.

(insert screenshot — clicking "Enter Grades" on a subject card)

#### Step 2: Select the Subject and Term

At the top of the grades view:

1. **Select the subject** from the dropdown menu. If you teach multiple subjects in this class, make sure you've selected the correct one.
2. **Select the term** (T1, T2, or T3) using the toggle buttons. Make sure you're entering grades for the correct grading period.

(insert screenshot — the subject dropdown and term toggle buttons)

> **Warning:** Entering grades for the wrong term or subject can cause issues. Always double-check that the correct subject and term are selected before entering grades.

#### Step 3: Enter Grades for Each Student

- Type a score from **0 to 100** in the input field next to each student's name.
- The input field will highlight in **red** if you enter a number outside the valid range (below 0 or above 100).
- If a grade already exists for a student, you'll see an **"Overwrite"** warning when you try to enter a new value — this is to prevent accidental changes.

(insert screenshot — the grade input table showing student names and grade fields)

#### Step 4: Use Bulk Fill (Optional)

For efficiency, you can use the bulk fill option:

- Click **"Fill All with Same Grade"** to apply one grade value to all students at once.
- This is useful for:
  - Participation grades (e.g., everyone gets 100 for participating)
  - Class activity grades (e.g., everyone gets the same score for a group activity)
  - Quick entries when most students received the same score

(insert screenshot — the "Fill All with Same Grade" button)

#### Step 5: Submit Your Grades

After entering all grades:

1. **Review your entries** — Scroll through the list to make sure all grades are correct.
2. Click **"Submit"** to save and submit the grades for admin review.

(insert screenshot — the Submit button at the bottom of the grade entry page)

### Grade Submission Workflow

Grades go through a multi-stage workflow:

```
Draft / In Progress → Submitted → Reviewed → Approved → Locked
```

| Status | What It Means | What You Can Do |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Draft / In Progress** | Grades are still being entered and edited | Continue entering or modifying grades. Your progress is saved automatically. |
| **Submitted** | Sent to admin for review | Read-only — you cannot edit until admin takes action. You can click "Reopen" to move back to Draft if needed. |
| **Reviewed** | Admin has reviewed the grades | Minor corrections may be requested. Wait for final approval. |
| **Approved** | Admin has approved your grades | Grades are finalized and visible to students and parents. |
| **Locked** | Grades are locked and cannot be changed | To edit, you must **Request Reopening** with a valid reason. |

### Viewing Grade Summary

1. Click the **"Manage"** card in the Grades tab to see a comprehensive table of all students.
2. The table shows each student's:
   - **Q1, Q2, Q3** — Individual quarter grades
   - **Final Grade** — The computed final grade
   - **Remarks** — Based on the grading scale (see below)

(insert screenshot — the Grade Summary table showing all students and their grades)

### Exporting Grades

From the Manage view, you can export grades in two formats:

- **CSV** — Downloads the grade data as a spreadsheet file (.csv) that you can open in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets. Useful for offline analysis or record-keeping.
- **SF10 PDF** — Generates the official Philippine report card format (SF10) as a PDF file. This is the standardized format used by the Department of Education.

(insert screenshot — the CSV and PDF export buttons in the Grade Summary view)

### Grading Remarks Scale

The portal automatically computes remarks based on the final grade:

| Marks Range | Remarks Label |
|-------------|---------------|
| 90–100 | Outstanding |
| 85–89 | Very Satisfactory |
| 80–84 | Satisfactory |
| 75–79 | Fairly Satisfactory |
| Below 75 | Did Not Meet Expectations |

### Requesting Reopening of Locked Grades

If grades are locked and you need to make corrections:

1. Open the **Grades** tab for the affected classroom.
2. Click **"Request Reopening"** button.
3. A modal will appear asking for a **reason** — provide a clear and specific explanation (e.g., "Entered wrong grade for Juan Dela Cruz — should be 85, not 75").
4. Submit the request. The admin will review and, if approved, unlock the grades for editing.

(insert screenshot — the Request Reopening button and the reason modal)

### Important Notes About Grading

- **Grading periods are time-limited.** Grade input is only available during open grading periods. If the period is closed, you'll see a locked screen with the message "Grading period is closed." Contact the admin if you need access outside the open period.
- **Save frequently.** The portal auto-saves your progress, but it's a good habit to check that your grades are saved before navigating away.
- **No duplicate grades.** Each student can only have one grade per subject per term. If you need to change a grade, edit the existing entry rather than creating a new one.

## 6. Uploading Learning Materials

**Where to go:** My Classes → Select a class → **Materials** tab.

Learning materials include lesson plans, worksheets, modules, assessments, and any other resources you want to share with your students.

### Step-by-Step: Uploading a Material

#### Step 1: Navigate to the Materials Tab

1. Open a classroom from **My Classes**.
2. Click the **Materials** tab at the top of the classroom page.

(insert screenshot — the Materials tab inside a classroom)

#### Step 2: Click the Upload Button

Click the **"Upload"** button in the Materials tab header. A modal (popup form) will appear.

(insert screenshot — the Upload button in the Materials tab header)

#### Step 3: Fill In the Material Details

Complete the upload form with the following fields:

| Field | Required? | Description | Example |
|-------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Title** | Yes | A clear, descriptive name for the material | "Grade 7 Math - Week 3 Lesson Plan" |
| **Description** | No | A brief description of what the material covers | "Lesson on algebraic expressions with examples and practice problems" |
| **Type** | Yes | The category of the material (select from dropdown) | DLP, DLL, Learning Module, Activity Sheet, Assessment, Other |
| **File** | Yes | The actual file to upload | Select from your computer (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, JPG, PNG) |
| **Term** | No | Which term this material belongs to (select from dropdown) | 1st Term, 2nd Term, or 3rd Term |
| **Week** | No | The week number for organization | "Week 3" |

(insert screenshot — the Upload Material modal with all form fields visible)

#### Step 4: Select Your File

1. Click the **"Choose File"** or **"Browse"** button in the file upload area.
2. Navigate to the file on your computer.
3. Select the file and click **"Open"**.
4. The file name will appear in the form, confirming it has been selected.

> **Supported file types:** PDF, DOCX, DOC, PPTX, PPT, XLSX, XLS, JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, WEBP. Maximum file size may vary — check with your admin if you need to upload very large files.

#### Step 5: Upload the Material

Click the **"Upload"** button at the bottom of the modal to save the material. The modal will close, and your new material will appear in the Materials list.

(insert screenshot — the material card appearing in the Materials list after upload)

### Material Types Explained

| Type | Full Name | Typical Use |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **DLP** | Daily Lesson Plan | Detailed lesson planning with objectives, procedures, and assessments. Required for DepEd compliance. |
| **DLL** | Daily Lesson Log | Quick logging of daily lessons taught. A lighter alternative to DLP. |
| **Module** | Learning Module | Student learning packets or self-study materials for independent learning. |
| **Activity** | Activity Sheet | Exercises, worksheets, and practice activities for students to complete. |
| **Assessment** | Assessment | Tests, quizzes, and exams used to evaluate student learning. |
| **Other** | Other | Any other resource that doesn't fit the above categories. |

### Managing Materials

- **Search:** Use the search bar at the top of the Materials tab to filter materials by title or description. Type a keyword and the list will update automatically.
- **View:** Click on a material card to view its details or download the file.
- **Delete:** Click the trash icon on a material card, then confirm the deletion in the confirmation dialog. Note that **deleted materials cannot be recovered**.

(insert screenshot — the search bar and material cards with the delete icon)

## 7. Posting Announcements

You can post announcements in two places: **school-wide** (visible to everyone) or **class-specific** (visible only to your students).

### Option A: School-Wide Announcement

Use this when you want to reach a broad audience — all students, parents, or faculty.

#### Step 1: Open the Announcements Page

Click **"Announcements"** in the sidebar under the "Communication" section.

(insert screenshot — the Announcements link in the sidebar)

#### Step 2: Start a New Announcement

Click the **"Create a school announcement..."** card at the top of the feed. The **Post Composer** modal will open.

(insert screenshot — the "Create a school announcement" card at the top of the announcements feed)

#### Step 3: Compose Your Announcement

The Post Composer has 3 tabs. Fill in each tab:

**Tab 1 — Compose:**

- **Title** (required): Enter a clear, descriptive title for your announcement. Maximum 150 characters. Make it concise but informative (e.g., "Scheduled Power Outage on March 15").
- **Content** (required): Write the main body of your announcement. You can use multiple paragraphs. Be clear and specific about what students/parents need to know.
- **Attachments** (optional): Click the attachment icon (📎) to attach files (PDF, DOC, XLS) or images (JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP) to provide additional context. You can attach multiple files.

(insert screenshot — the Compose tab with Title, Content, and attachment options)

**Tab 2 — Audience:**

- **Target Audience** (required): Choose who should see this announcement:
  - **All Users** — Everyone (students, teachers, parents)
  - **Students** — Only students
  - **Faculty** — Only teachers and staff
  - **Parents** — Only parent accounts
- **Target Sections** (optional): If you want to narrow the audience further, select specific classrooms/sections from the dropdown. Leave this empty to send to all users in the selected audience.

(insert screenshot — the Audience tab with Target Audience dropdown and Target Sections multi-select)

**Tab 3 — Settings:**

Configure the announcement's behavior and visibility:

- **Category** (required): Choose the topic category that best describes your announcement:
  - General, Academics, Events, Examinations, Guidance, Sports, Emergency, Holiday, System
- **Priority**: Set how urgent the announcement is:
  - **Normal** — Standard announcement (default)
  - **Important** — Highlighted with a different color to draw attention
  - **Urgent** — Displayed prominently with an alert-style banner
- **Status**: Choose when to publish:
  - **Publish now** — Posts the announcement immediately
  - **Save as draft** — Saves the announcement without publishing. You can come back to edit and publish later.
- **Pin to top**: Check this to keep the announcement at the top of the feed so it's always visible, regardless of newer posts.
- **Show on public website**: Check this to display the announcement on the school's public homepage (visible even to people who aren't logged in to the portal).
- **Schedule Post** (optional): Set a future date and time to auto-publish the announcement. The announcement will be held as a draft until the scheduled time.
- **Expires** (optional): Set a date and time after which the announcement will be automatically archived and hidden from the feed.

(insert screenshot — the Settings tab with all configuration options)

#### Step 4: Post the Announcement

Review all three tabs to make sure everything is correct, then click **"Post"** to publish the announcement. If you chose "Save as draft," click **"Save Draft"** instead.

(insert screenshot — the Post button at the bottom of the Post Composer)

### Option B: Class-Specific Announcement

Use this when you want to communicate with only the students in one of your classes.

1. Open a class from **My Classes**.
2. Click the **Stream** tab.
3. You'll see a text area at the top of the Stream. Click on it to start typing.

   (insert screenshot — the Stream tab with the announcement text area)

4. Write your announcement content in the text area.
5. Optionally add a **title** by clicking the "Add title" option.
6. Optionally **attach files** by clicking the attachment icon (📎). You can attach up to 5 files.
7. Click **"Post"** to publish the announcement to your class.

(insert screenshot — the announcement text area with title, content, and attachment options)

### Managing Your Announcements

After posting, you can manage your announcements by hovering over them:

- **Edit** — Click the pencil icon (✏️) to modify the content. A modal will open with the current content, allowing you to make changes.
- **Delete** — Click the trash icon (🗑️) and confirm to permanently remove the announcement. **This action cannot be undone.**
- **Pin/Unpin** — Click the pin icon (📌) to toggle pinning to the top of the feed. Pinned announcements always appear at the top regardless of when they were posted.
- **Archive** — Click the archive icon (📦) to hide the announcement from the active feed. Archived announcements can be viewed separately but are not shown in the main feed.

## 8. Adding a Student to a Class

Students are added to classes through the **Enrollment Hub**. Teachers can view students in the **People** tab, but the actual enrollment process is handled through the enrollment system.

### Method 1: Assigning an Approved Applicant to a Section

Use this when an applicant has already been approved and you need to assign them to a specific class.

1. Click **"Enrollment"** in the sidebar (or go to `/enrollment-hub`).
2. In the **Applications** tab, find the approved applicant using the search bar or filters.
3. Click **"Assign Section"** on the application row.
4. A dropdown will appear showing available classrooms with their current enrollment count vs. capacity (e.g., "Grade 7-Rizal — 35/45 students"). Select the appropriate classroom.
5. Confirm the assignment.

(insert screenshot — the Assign Section dropdown showing classroom options with enrollment counts)

### Method 2: Enrolling a Student from an Approved Application

Use this when you want to enroll a student and optionally link a parent account at the same time.

1. In the Enrollment Hub → **Applications** tab, find an application with **Approved** status.
2. Click **"Enroll"** on the row.
3. An enrollment modal will appear with the following fields:
   - **Assign Section:** Select the classroom for the student from the dropdown.
   - **Parent Email:** (Optional) Enter the parent's email address to link their parent account to the student. The parent will receive a notification and can then track their child's progress.
4. Click **"Enroll Now"**.
5. A confirmation modal will appear showing:
   - The student's **email address** (their login credential)
   - Their **LRN** (Learner Reference Number)
   - A **temporary password** (they must change this on first login)
   - Their **assigned section**

> **Important:** Save the student's credentials securely (write them down or screenshot them) and communicate them to the student/parent. This is the only time the temporary password is shown.

(insert screenshot — the enrollment confirmation modal showing student credentials)

### Method 3: Directly Adding Students to a Classroom (Bulk)

Use this when you need to add multiple students to a class at once.

1. Click **"Enrollment"** in the sidebar.
2. Switch to the **"Enroll Students"** tab.
3. You'll see a grid of classroom cards. Click on the classroom you want to add students to.
4. The classroom card expands to show its current student list.
5. Click **"Add Students"** button.
6. A modal will appear with a searchable list of all students who are not yet enrolled in this classroom.
   - **Search** for students by name, LRN, or email using the search bar at the top.
   - **Check the boxes** next to the students you want to add.
   - Or use **"Select All"** to add all listed students at once.
7. Click **"Enroll N Student(s)"** to confirm the enrollment.

(insert screenshot — the Add Students modal with the searchable list and checkboxes)

### Changing a Student's Section

If a student needs to move to a different class or section:

1. Go to Enrollment Hub → **Enroll Students** tab → select the classroom the student is currently in.
2. Find the student in the enrolled list.
3. Click **"Change Section"** on the student's row.
4. Select the new classroom from the dropdown. The dropdown shows the current enrollment count of the target classroom so you can check for overcrowding.
5. Confirm the transfer.

(insert screenshot — the Change Section dropdown with classroom options)

### Withdrawing a Student

If a student is leaving the school:

1. Find the student in the enrolled list (Enrollment Hub → Enroll Students → select classroom).
2. Click **"Withdraw"** on the student's row.
3. A withdrawal form will appear with:
   - **Reason Type** (required): Select from the dropdown:
     - **Transferred Out** — Student moved to another school
     - **Withdrawn** — Family chose to withdraw the student
     - **Dropped** — Student stopped attending without formal withdrawal
     - **Other** — Any other reason
   - **Reason** (required): Enter a detailed explanation. Be specific for record-keeping purposes (e.g., "Family relocated to Cebu City").
4. Confirm the withdrawal.

> **Note:** Withdrawal is a serious action. The student will be removed from all classes and will no longer have access to the portal. Make sure this is the correct action before confirming.

## 9. Enrollment

The enrollment system manages the full lifecycle of a student application: from initial submission through review, approval, and final enrollment.

### Viewing Enrollment Applications

1. Click **"Enrollment"** in the sidebar → `/enrollment-hub`.
2. The **Applications** tab shows:
   - **6 stat cards** at the top: Total, Pending, Under Review, Approved, Enrolled, Rejected — each card shows the count for that status. Click any card to filter the table by that status.
   - **Filters** at the top of the table:
     - **Search** — Search by student name, email, or enrollment number
     - **Status** — Filter by application status
     - **Grade Level** — Filter by grade level (Grade 7, Grade 8, etc.)
     - **Enrollment Type** — Filter by type (New, Old, Transferee)
     - **School Year** — Filter by school year
   - **Application table** with action buttons for each row

(insert screenshot — the Enrollment Hub showing stat cards, filters, and the applications table)

### Application Statuses Explained

| Status | Meaning | What Happens Next |
|--------|---------|-------------------|
| **Pending** | New application, awaiting staff review | Staff can start a review |
| **Under Review** | Being actively reviewed by school staff | Staff can request documents or approve/reject |
| **Pending Requirements** | Additional documents are needed from the applicant | Applicant must submit the missing documents |
| **Approved** | Application approved, ready to enroll | Admin can assign a section and enroll the student |
| **Enrolled** | Student is officially enrolled | Student can access the portal and attend classes |
| **Rejected** | Application was denied | Applicant is notified of the rejection |
| **Cancelled** | Applicant cancelled their own application | No further action needed |
| **Withdrawn** | Enrolled student withdrew from the school | Student is removed from classes |

### Processing an Application (Complete Workflow)

#### Step 1: Start Review

1. Click on a **Pending** application in the table.
2. The application details will expand or open in a modal.
3. Click **"Start Review"** — the status changes from **Pending** to **Under Review**.

(insert screenshot — the Start Review button on a pending application)

#### Step 2: Request Documents (If Needed)

If you need additional documents from the applicant:

1. Click **"Request Documents"** on the application.
2. A form will appear where you can:
   - **Select required document types** (e.g., Birth Certificate, Report Card, Form 138, Good Moral Certificate, etc.)
   - **Add a message** explaining exactly what's needed and any deadlines
3. The applicant will be notified automatically via the portal and (if enabled) email.

(insert screenshot — the Request Documents form with document type checkboxes and message field)

#### Step 3: Approve the Application

Once you've verified all documents and information:

1. Click **"Approve"** on the reviewed application.
2. Optionally add **remarks** (e.g., "All documents verified. Student is eligible for enrollment.").
3. The status changes to **Approved**.
4. The applicant receives a notification that their application has been approved.

(insert screenshot — the Approve button and remarks field)

#### Step 4: Reject the Application (If Not Approved)

If the application cannot be approved:

1. Click **"Reject"** on the application.
2. Enter a **reason** (required — this is sent to the applicant so they understand why their application was denied).
3. The status changes to **Rejected** and the applicant is notified.

#### Step 5: Enroll the Student

Once the application is **Approved**, you can enroll the student:

1. Click **"Enroll"** on the approved application.
2. Assign a **section** (classroom) from the dropdown.
3. Optionally enter a **parent email** to link the parent's account.
4. Click **"Enroll Now"**.
5. **Important:** Save the student's credentials (email, temporary password, LRN) — you'll need to share these with the student/parent.

(insert screenshot — the Enroll modal with section assignment and parent email fields)

### Bulk Actions

Select multiple applications using the **checkboxes** on each row. A bulk actions bar will appear at the bottom of the table:

- **Approve All** — Approve all selected applications at once.
- **Reject All** — Reject all selected applications (you'll need to enter a reason for each).

## 13. Tips & Troubleshooting

### General Tips

- **Save frequently** when taking attendance or entering grades. Click "Save" often to avoid losing your work if something unexpected happens (browser crash, internet disconnection, etc.).
- **Check grading deadlines** on the Grade Dashboard. Overdue submissions are highlighted in red — don't wait until the last minute.
- **Use "All Present"** as a starting point for attendance, then mark exceptions (absent, late, excused) — this is much faster than marking each student individually.
- **Attach files** when posting announcements to provide additional context or supporting documents.
- **Use the search bar** in My Classes, Materials, and the Enrollment Hub to quickly find specific students or files.
- **Enable push notifications** to stay updated on grade submissions, attendance approvals, and important announcements — even when you're not actively using the portal.
- **Use keyboard shortcuts** to speed up common tasks:
  - `Enter` — Confirm inline grade edits
  - `Escape` — Cancel inline editing and discard changes

### Common Issues and Solutions

| Issue | Possible Cause | Solution |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| Cannot enter grades | The grading period is not open or has already closed | Check the grading period dates on the Grade Dashboard. If the period shows as closed, contact the admin to request access. |
| Attendance shows locked | Admin has already approved/locked the attendance | If you need to make changes, click **"Reopen"** to move it back to Draft, make your corrections, then re-submit. |
| Student not appearing in class | The student may not be enrolled in that section | Verify the student's enrollment via Enrollment Hub → Enroll Students tab. If the student is not enrolled, follow the enrollment process. |
| Material upload fails | File may be too large or in an unsupported format | Check the file size and format. Supported formats: PDF, DOCX, DOC, PPTX, PPT, XLSX, XLS, JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, WEBP. Contact admin if you need to upload a larger file. |
| Cannot post announcement | You may not have the correct permissions | Ensure you are logged in as Faculty/Admin. Students have read-only access and cannot post. If you're a teacher and still can't post, contact the admin. |
| Grade submission button disabled | You haven't entered any grades yet | You must enter at least one grade before the Submit button becomes active. Enter grades for at least one student, then the button will enable. |
| "No classes today" message | The selected date may be a school holiday or weekend | Check the school calendar for holidays and non-school days. Try navigating to a different date using the date picker. |
| Dashboard shows no classes | Your account may not be assigned to any classes yet | Contact the admin to assign you to classrooms and subjects. |
| Notifications not appearing | Push notifications may not be enabled | Go to Notifications → Settings gear → Toggle Push Notifications ON and allow the browser permission prompt. |
| Cannot see student grades | The grading period may not be open for your subject | Check the Grade Dashboard for active grading periods. If no periods are shown, contact the admin. |
| Page loads slowly | Internet connection may be slow or the server may be busy | Try refreshing the page. If the issue persists, check your internet connection and try again later. |
| Login fails repeatedly | Password may be incorrect or account may be locked | Try the "Forgot Password" link to reset your password. If that doesn't work, contact the admin to verify your account status. |
