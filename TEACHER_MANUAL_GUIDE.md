## 1. Logging In

Before you can use the portal, you need to log in with your teacher account.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open your browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari recommended).
2. **Go to the KNHS School Portal website.** Ask your school administrator for the correct URL if you don't know it.
3. **Click the "Login" button** located at the top-right corner of the homepage.
4. **Enter your email address and password** — these were provided to you by the school administrator when your account was created.
5. **Click "Sign In."** You will be automatically redirected to your Dashboard.

### What If You Can't Log In?

- **Forgot your password?** Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page and follow the instructions to reset it.
- **Account not working?** Contact the school IT administrator to verify your account is active.
- **Wrong role?** Make sure you're logging in with your teacher account, not a student or parent account.

> **Note:** Your account role is set to **Faculty/Staff**. This means you'll see teacher-specific pages in the sidebar, and you won't have access to admin-only features like user management or system settings.

## 2. Dashboard Overview

After logging in, the **Dashboard** is your home base. It gives you a quick snapshot of your classes, recent activity, and shortcuts to the most-used features.

### Sidebar Navigation

The sidebar on the left side of the screen organizes all the features available to you. Here's what each section contains:

| Section | Menu Items | What You'll Find |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Workspaces** | Dashboard, My Classes, My Schedule | Your main workspace — view classes, schedules, and the dashboard home |
| **Teaching** | Grade Submission, Attendance Dashboard, My Compliance | Tools for managing grades, attendance records, and compliance documents |
| **Communication** | Messages, Announcements, Notifications | Send and receive messages, post announcements, and view alerts |
| **Resources** | Enrollment, Schedules, School Forms, Calendar | Access enrollment tools, view schedules, download school forms, and check the calendar |
| **Account** | Settings | Update your profile, change password, or adjust notification preferences |

### Dashboard Widgets

On the Dashboard home page, you'll see:

- **Quick Stats** — A summary of your classes, students, and pending tasks
- **Upcoming Schedule** — Your next classes and periods for the day
- **Recent Announcements** — The latest school-wide or class announcements
- **Quick Links** — Shortcut buttons to jump to Attendance, Grade Submission, or My Classes

## 3. My Classes

**Route:** `/my-classes`

"My Classes" is where you manage everything related to the classes you teach. Think of it as your personal teaching workspace.

### Viewing Your Classes

1. Click **"My Classes"** in the sidebar.
2. You'll see a grid of **classroom cards**, one for each class assigned to you.
3. Each card shows:
   - **Classroom name and grade level** — e.g., "Grade 7 - Rizal"
   - **Number of subjects** you teach in that class
   - **Number of enrolled students** currently in the class

### Opening a Classroom

Click on any classroom card to open it. Inside each classroom, you'll find **7 tabs** that cover different aspects of class management:

| Tab | Purpose | What You Can Do |
|-----|---------|-----------------|
| **Stream** | Class announcements and feed | View and post class-specific announcements, see the activity feed |
| **Materials** | Learning materials | Upload, organize, and manage files like lesson plans, worksheets, and modules |
| **People** | Student roster | View the list of enrolled students, their statuses, and contact info |
| **Attendance** | Daily attendance | Mark attendance for each class period |
| **History** | Past records | Review attendance records from previous dates |
| **Grades** | Grade management | Input, edit, and manage student grades for each subject |
| **Analytics** | Performance insights | View charts and summaries of class performance trends |

> **Tip:** The tabs are always in the same order, so you can quickly find what you need once you're familiar with the layout.

## 4. Taking Attendance

Attendance tracking is one of your most important daily tasks. The portal supports two types of attendance: **schedule-based** (for subject teachers) and **class-level/homeroom** (for advisory teachers).

### Three Ways to Access Attendance

You can reach the attendance system from multiple entry points:

1. **Attendance Dashboard** (`/attendance-dashboard`) — Shows an overview of all your periods for any day, with completion status indicators.
2. **My Classes** → Select a class → **Attendance** tab — Shows your scheduled periods for that specific class.
3. **Direct link from the Dashboard** — Click any period card on the dashboard to go straight to attendance entry.

### Step-by-Step: Taking Schedule-Based Attendance

This is the most common workflow for subject teachers who have a set schedule.

1. **Open the Attendance Dashboard** from the sidebar menu.

2. **Navigate to the right date** using the arrow buttons on the top of the calendar, or click the **date picker** to jump to a specific date. Click **"Today"** to quickly return to the current date.

3. **Review your scheduled periods** — Each card on the dashboard represents one class period. You'll see:
   - The **subject name** and **classroom** (e.g., "Mathematics - Grade 7-Rizal")
   - The **time period** (e.g., 8:00 AM – 9:00 AM)
   - The **student count** and how many have been recorded so far
   - A **completion status** badge: "Completed" (green) or "Pending" (amber)

4. **Click a period card** to open the attendance entry screen for that specific subject and time slot.

5. **Mark attendance for each student:**
   - For each student, click one of the four status buttons:
     - **Present** (green checkmark) — Student is present
     - **Absent** (red X) — Student is absent
     - **Late** (amber clock) — Student arrived after the start time
     - **Excused** (blue shield) — Student is excused (e.g., medical leave)
   - Optionally, add **Remarks** in the text field below each student (e.g., "Sick leave" or "Family emergency")

6. **Use Bulk Actions** to speed up the process:
   - Click **"All Present"** to mark every student in the class as Present at once — great as a starting point, then just change the exceptions.
   - Click **"Clear"** to reset all marks if you need to start over.

7. **Save your work:**
   - Click **"Save"** to save as a draft — you can come back and edit later before submitting.
   - Click **"Submit"** to finalize and send the attendance to the admin for review.

### Time-Window Access

- Teachers can mark attendance starting **15 minutes before** the scheduled period begins.
- Attendance can be marked until **5:00 PM** (end of the school day).
- **Admins** can mark attendance at any time without these restrictions.

### Dual Attendance Types

| Type | When Used | Description |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Schedule-Based** | Subject teachers (e.g., Math, English, Science teachers) | Marks attendance for a specific subject period that is linked to your daily schedule |
| **Class-Level (Homeroom)** | Advisory teachers (homeroom advisers) | Marks attendance for the entire class at once, without being tied to a specific schedule — used for homeroom periods |

### Attendance Status Workflow

Attendance goes through a simple workflow once submitted:

```
Draft → Submitted → (Admin reviews) → Approved / Locked
```

| Status | What It Means | What You Can Do |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Draft** | Your attendance is still being edited | Continue marking or editing student statuses |
| **Submitted** | Sent to admin for review | You can click **"Reopen"** to move it back to Draft if you need to make changes |
| **Locked** | Admin has approved and finalized | No further edits are possible |

### Viewing Attendance History

To review past attendance records:

1. Open a classroom from **My Classes**.
2. Click the **History** tab.
3. You'll see a list of past attendance records organized by date. Click any date to expand and see the period-by-period details.

### Student Calendar View

Students can view their own attendance as a **monthly calendar** with color-coded dates. As a teacher, you can see this view from the Attendance History tab:

- **Color-coded dates** — Each date is colored based on the attendance status for that day.
- **Click any date** to expand and see period-by-period details.
- **Toggle between Calendar and List views** using the view switcher at the top.
- **Filter by month** using the navigation arrows.

## 5. Grading

### Accessing the Grade Dashboard

1. Click **"Grade Submission"** in the sidebar, or navigate directly to `/teacher-grade-dashboard`.
2. You'll see the Grade Dashboard with the following:
   - **Active grading period** info with a countdown timer showing how much time remains
   - **Cards for each subject/classroom** you teach, showing grading progress (e.g., "3 of 25 grades entered")
   - **Filter buttons** at the top: Pending, Submitted, Overdue, Approved/Locked, All — click any filter to narrow the view

### Step-by-Step: Inputting Grades

1. From the Grade Dashboard, click **"Enter Grades"** on the card for the subject/classroom you want to grade.
   - This opens the **Grades** tab inside My Classes for that specific classroom.

2. **Select the subject** from the dropdown menu at the top of the grades view.

3. **Select the term** (T1, T2, or T3) using the toggle buttons. Make sure you're entering grades for the correct grading period.

4. **Enter grades for each student:**
   - Type a score from **0 to 100** in the input field next to each student's name.
   - If a grade already exists for a student, you'll see an **"Overwrite"** warning when you try to enter a new value — this is to prevent accidental changes.

5. **Bulk fill** (optional, for efficiency):
   - Click **"Fill All with Same Grade"** to apply one grade value to all students at once.
   - This is useful for quick entries like participation grades or when all students received the same score.

6. Click **"Submit"** to save and submit the grades for admin review.

### Grade Submission Workflow

Grades go through a multi-stage workflow:

```
Draft / In Progress → Submitted → Reviewed → Approved → Locked
```

| Status | What It Means | What You Can Do |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Draft / In Progress** | Grades are still being entered and edited | Continue entering or modifying grades |
| **Submitted** | Sent to admin for review | Read-only — you cannot edit until admin takes action |
| **Approved** | Admin has approved your grades | Grades are finalized and visible to students/parents |
| **Locked** | Grades are locked and cannot be changed | To edit, you must **Request Reopening** with a valid reason |

### Viewing Grade Summary

1. Click the **"Manage"** card in the Grades tab to see a comprehensive table of all students.
2. The table shows each student's **Q1, Q2, Q3, Final Grade**, and **Remarks**.

### Grading Remarks Scale

| Marks Range | Remarks Label |
|-------------|---------------|
| 90–100 | Outstanding |
| 85–89 | Very Satisfactory |
| 80–84 | Satisfactory |
| 75–79 | Fairly Satisfactory |
| Below 75 | Did Not Meet Expectations |

### Important Notes About Grading

- **Grading periods are time-limited.** Grade input is only available during open grading periods. If the period is closed, you'll see a locked screen — contact the admin if you need access.
- **Export options:** You can export grades as **CSV** (for spreadsheets) or **SF10 PDF** (the official Philippine report card format) from the Manage view.
- **Requesting reopening:** If grades are locked and you need to make corrections, click **"Request Reopening"** and provide a clear reason (e.g., "Entered wrong grade for Juan Dela Cruz").

## 6. Uploading Learning Materials

**Where to go:** My Classes → Select a class → **Materials** tab.

Learning materials include lesson plans, worksheets, modules, assessments, and any other resources you want to share with your students.

### Step-by-Step: Uploading a Material

1. Click the **"Upload"** button in the Materials tab header.
2. A upload form will appear. Fill in the fields:

| Field | Required? | Description | Example |
|-------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Title** | Yes | A clear name for the material | "Grade 7 Math - Week 3 Lesson Plan" |
| **Description** | No | A brief description of what the material covers | "Lesson on algebraic expressions with examples" |
| **Type** | Yes | The category of the material | DLP, DLL, Learning Module, Activity Sheet, Assessment, Other |
| **File** | Yes | The actual file to upload (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, etc.) | Select from your computer |
| **Term** | No | Which term this material belongs to | 1st Term, 2nd Term, or 3rd Term |
| **Week** | No | The week number for organization | "Week 3" |

3. Click **"Upload"** in the modal to save the material.

### Material Types Explained

| Type | Full Name | Typical Use |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **DLP** | Daily Lesson Plan | Detailed lesson planning with objectives, procedures, and assessments |
| **DLL** | Daily Lesson Log | Quick logging of daily lessons taught |
| **Module** | Learning Module | Student learning packets or self-study materials |
| **Activity** | Activity Sheet | Exercises, worksheets, and practice activities |
| **Assessment** | Assessment | Tests, quizzes, and exams |
| **Other** | Other | Any other resource that doesn't fit the above categories |

### Managing Materials

- **Search:** Use the search bar at the top of the Materials tab to filter materials by title or description.
- **Delete:** Click the trash icon on a material card, then confirm the deletion. Note that deleted materials cannot be recovered.

## 7. Posting Announcements

You can post announcements in two places: **school-wide** (visible to everyone) or **class-specific** (visible only to your students).

### Option A: School-Wide Announcement

Use this when you want to reach a broad audience — all students, parents, or faculty.

1. Click **"Announcements"** in the sidebar.
2. Click the **"Create a school announcement..."** card at the top of the feed.
3. The **Post Composer** opens with 3 tabs:

   **Tab 1 — Compose:**
   - Enter a **Title** (maximum 150 characters) — make it clear and descriptive.
   - Write the **Content** — the main body of your announcement.
   - Optionally **attach files** (PDF, DOC, XLS) or **images** (JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP) to provide additional context.

   **Tab 2 — Audience:**
   - **Target Audience:** Choose who should see this announcement:
     - **All Users** — Everyone (students, teachers, parents)
     - **Students** — Only students
     - **Faculty** — Only teachers and staff
     - **Parents** — Only parent accounts
   - **Target Sections (optional):** Select specific classrooms/sections to narrow the audience further.

   **Tab 3 — Settings:**
   - **Category:** Choose the topic category (General, Academics, Events, Examinations, Guidance, Sports, Emergency, Holiday, System).
   - **Priority:** Set how urgent the announcement is (Normal, Important, or Urgent).
   - **Status:** Choose "Publish now" to post immediately, or "Save as draft" to review later.
   - **Pin to top:** Check this to keep the announcement at the top of the feed so it's always visible.
   - **Show on public website:** Check this to display the announcement on the school's public homepage (visible even to people who aren't logged in).
   - **Schedule Post:** Set a future date and time to auto-publish the announcement.
   - **Expires:** Set a date and time after which the announcement will be automatically archived.

4. Click **"Post"** to publish the announcement.

### Option B: Class-Specific Announcement

Use this when you want to communicate with only the students in one of your classes.

1. Open a class from **My Classes**.
2. Click the **Stream** tab.
3. Type your announcement content in the text area.
4. Optionally add a **title** and **file attachments** (up to 5 files).
5. Click **"Post"**.

### Managing Your Announcements

After posting, you can manage your announcements by hovering over them:

- **Edit** — Click the pencil icon to modify the content.
- **Delete** — Click the trash icon and confirm to permanently remove the announcement.
- **Pin/Unpin** — Click the pin icon to toggle pinning to the top of the feed.
- **Archive** — Click the archive icon to hide the announcement from the active feed.

## 8. Adding a Student to a Class

Students are added to classes through the **Enrollment Hub**. Teachers can view students in the **People** tab, but the actual enrollment process is handled through the enrollment system.

### Method 1: Assigning an Approved Applicant to a Section

Use this when an applicant has already been approved and you need to assign them to a specific class.

1. Click **"Enrollment"** in the sidebar (or go to `/enrollment-hub`).
2. In the **Applications** tab, find the approved applicant.
3. Click **"Assign Section"** on the application row.
4. Select a **classroom** from the dropdown — it will show the current enrollment count vs. the classroom's capacity so you can avoid overcrowding.
5. Confirm the assignment.

### Method 2: Enrolling a Student from an Approved Application

Use this when you want to enroll a student and optionally link a parent account at the same time.

1. In the Enrollment Hub → **Applications** tab, find an **Approved** application.
2. Click **"Enroll"** on the row.
3. In the enrollment modal:
   - **Assign Section:** Select the classroom for the student.
   - **Parent Email:** (Optional) Enter the parent's email address to link their parent account to the student.
4. Click **"Enroll Now"**.
5. A confirmation modal will appear showing the student's:
   - Email address (their login credential)
   - LRN (Learner Reference Number)
   - Temporary password (they must change this on first login)
   - Assigned section

> **Important:** Save the student's credentials securely and communicate them to the student/parent.

### Method 3: Directly Adding Students to a Classroom (Bulk)

Use this when you need to add multiple students to a class at once.

1. Click **"Enrollment"** in the sidebar.
2. Switch to the **"Enroll Students"** tab.
3. Click on a **classroom card** to see its current student list.
4. Click **"Add Students"**.
5. In the modal:
   - **Search** for students by name, LRN, or email.
   - **Check the boxes** next to the students you want to add.
   - Or use **"Select All"** to add all listed students at once.
6. Click **"Enroll N Student(s)"** to confirm the enrollment.

### Changing a Student's Section

If a student needs to move to a different class or section:

1. Go to Enrollment Hub → **Enroll Students** tab → select the classroom the student is currently in.
2. Find the student in the enrolled list.
3. Click **"Change Section"**.
4. Select the new classroom from the dropdown.
5. Confirm the transfer.

### Withdrawing a Student

If a student is leaving the school:

1. Find the student in the enrolled list.
2. Click **"Withdraw"**.
3. Select a **reason type** from the dropdown:
   - **Transferred Out** — Student moved to another school
   - **Withdrawn** — Family chose to withdraw
   - **Dropped** — Student stopped attending without formal withdrawal
   - **Other** — Any other reason
4. Enter a **reason** (required — be specific for record-keeping).
5. Confirm the withdrawal.

## 9. Enrollment

The enrollment system manages the full lifecycle of a student application: from initial submission through review, approval, and final enrollment.

### Viewing Enrollment Applications

1. Click **"Enrollment"** in the sidebar → `/enrollment-hub`.
2. The **Applications** tab shows:
   - **6 stat cards** at the top: Total, Pending, Under Review, Approved, Enrolled, Rejected — each card shows the count for that status.
   - **Filters** at the top of the table: Search (by name, email, or enrollment number), Status, Grade Level, Enrollment Type, and School Year.
   - **Application table** with action buttons for each row.

### Application Statuses Explained

| Status | Meaning | What Happens Next |
|--------|---------|-------------------|
| **Pending** | New application, awaiting staff review | Staff can start a review |
| **Under Review** | Being actively reviewed by school staff | Staff can request documents or approve/reject |
| **Pending Requirements** | Additional documents are needed | Applicant must submit the missing documents |
| **Approved** | Application approved, ready to enroll | Admin can assign a section and enroll the student |
| **Enrolled** | Student is officially enrolled | Student can access the portal and attend classes |
| **Rejected** | Application was denied | Applicant is notified of the rejection |
| **Cancelled** | Applicant cancelled their own application | No further action needed |
| **Withdrawn** | Enrolled student withdrew from the school | Student is removed from classes |

### Processing an Application (Complete Workflow)

1. **Start Review:**
   - Click on a **Pending** application.
   - Click **"Start Review"** — the status changes to **Under Review**.

2. **Request Documents (if needed):**
   - Click **"Request Documents"** on the application.
   - Select the required document types (e.g., Birth Certificate, Report Card, Form 138, etc.).
   - Add a message explaining exactly what's needed.
   - The applicant will be notified automatically.

3. **Approve the Application:**
   - Click **"Approve"** on a reviewed application.
   - Optionally add remarks (e.g., "All documents verified").
   - Status changes to **Approved**.

4. **Reject the Application (if not approved):**
   - Click **"Reject"** on the application.
   - Enter a **reason** (required — this is sent to the applicant).
   - The applicant is notified of the rejection.

5. **Enroll the Student:**
   - Once the application is **Approved**, click **"Enroll"**.
   - Assign a **section** (classroom) from the dropdown.
   - Optionally enter a **parent email** to link the parent's account.
   - Click **"Enroll Now"**.
   - **Important:** Save the student's credentials (email, temporary password, LRN) — you'll need to share these with the student/parent.

### Bulk Actions

- Select multiple applications using the **checkboxes** on each row.
- Use the **bulk actions bar** at the bottom:
  - **Approve All** — Approve all selected applications at once.
  - **Reject All** — Reject all selected (you'll need to enter a reason for each).

### Exporting Enrollment Data

- Click **CSV** to download the application list as a spreadsheet for offline review.
- Click **PDF** to generate a formatted PDF report for records.

## 10. Tips & Troubleshooting

### General Tips

- **Save frequently** when taking attendance or entering grades. Click "Save" often to avoid losing your work if something unexpected happens.
- **Check grading deadlines** on the Grade Dashboard. Overdue submissions are highlighted in red — don't wait until the last minute.
- **Use "All Present"** as a starting point, then mark exceptions (absent, late, excused) — this is much faster than marking each student individually.
- **Attach files** when posting announcements to provide additional context or supporting documents.
- **Use the search bar** in My Classes and the Materials tab to quickly find specific students or files.

### Common Issues and Solutions

| Issue | Possible Cause | Solution |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| Cannot enter grades | The grading period is not open or has already closed | Check the grading period dates on the Grade Dashboard. Contact the admin if you need access outside the open period. |
| Attendance shows locked | Admin has already approved/locked the attendance | If you need to make changes, click **"Reopen"** to move it back to Draft, then re-submit. |
| Student not appearing in class | The student may not be enrolled in that section | Verify the student's enrollment via the Enrollment Hub → Enroll Students tab. |
| Material upload fails | File may be too large or in an unsupported format | Check the file size and format. Most common file types (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, JPG, PNG) are supported. |
| Cannot post announcement | You may not have the correct permissions | Ensure you are logged in as Faculty/Admin. Students have read-only access and cannot post. |
| Grade submission button disabled | You haven't entered any grades yet | You must enter at least one grade before the Submit button becomes active. |
| "No classes today" message | The selected date may be a school holiday | Check the school calendar for holidays and non-school days. |

### Keyboard Shortcuts

- **Enter** — Confirm inline grade edits
- **Escape** — Cancel inline editing and discard changes

### Getting Help

- **Technical issues** (bugs, errors, system downtime): Contact the school IT administrator.
- **Account access problems** (forgot password, locked account): Visit the **Settings** page to update your profile or request a password reset.
- **Feature questions** (how to do something in the portal): Refer to this manual or ask your school administrator.
