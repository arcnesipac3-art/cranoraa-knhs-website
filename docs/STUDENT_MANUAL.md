## 1. Logging In

> **Go to:** Portal website → Login

1. Enter your **Student ID / LRN** (12 digits).
2. Enter your **password**.
3. Select **Student**.
4. Click **Log In**.

**First time?** Use the temp password from enrollment. You'll be asked to change it.

**Forgot password?** Click "Forgot Password?" → enter your email → follow the reset link.

## 2. Dashboard

> **Go to:** Sidebar → Dashboard

Your home page. Shows:

- **Summary cards** — enrolled subjects, grade average, attendance rate
- **Today's schedule** — your classes for today (current class highlighted green)
- **Quick links** — shortcuts to My Classes, Schedule, Grades, Attendance

**Tip:** Click any class in Today's Schedule to open it.

---

## 3. My Classes

> **Go to:** Sidebar → My Classes

### Open a Class

1. Click **My Classes** in the sidebar.
2. Click on a class card to open it.

### Inside a Class

| Tab | What's There |
|---|---|
| **Stream** | Teacher announcements, comments, file attachments |
| **Materials** | Learning materials (modules, worksheets) — search by quarter/week |
| **Grades** | Your grades per subject, semester averages, performance level |
| **Attendance** | Calendar with color dots (green=present, red=absent, amber=late, blue=excused) |
| **People** | List of classmates and teachers |

## 4. Schedule

> **Go to:** Sidebar → My Schedule

Your weekly timetable.

- **Grid view** — days on one side, time slots on the other

## 5. Attendance

> **Go to:** Sidebar → Attendance

### Check Your Attendance

1. Go to **Attendance**.
2. See the **calendar** — colored dots show your status each day.
3. Switch to **list view** for details.
4. Use the **date filter** to find specific records.

### Color Code

- 🟢 Green = Present
- 🟡 Amber = Late
- 🔴 Red = Absent
- 🔵 Blue = Excused

## 6. Grades

> **Go to:** Sidebar → My Classes → Grades tab

### Check Grades

1. Go to **My Classes** → select a class.
2. Click the **Grades** tab.
3. Use the **quarter filter** to focus on a specific quarter.

### What You See

- Grades per subject for each quarter
- Semester averages
- Overall average
- Performance badge:
  - **Outstanding** (90–100)
  - **Very Satisfactory** (85–89)
  - **Satisfactory** (80–84)
  - **Fairly Satisfactory** (75–79)
  - **Did Not Meet** (below 75)

### Download Report Card

> **Go to:** Sidebar → School Forms → SF9

1. Click **SF9**.
2. View your report card.
3. Click **Download PDF**.

## 7. Quizzes

> **Go to:** Sidebar → My Classes → Quizzes tab

### Take a Quiz

1. Go to **My Classes** → select a class → **Quizzes** tab.
2. Find a quiz with **Available Now** badge.
3. Click **Start Quiz**.
4. Read the instructions.
5. Answer questions using **Next/Previous** buttons.
6. Click **Mark for Review** on questions to revisit.
7. Click **Submit** when done.

### Question Types

- **Multiple Choice** — click one option
- **True/False** — select True or False
- **Identification** — type your answer
- **Essay** — type in the text area

### Rules

- Timer counts down — auto-submits when time runs out
- Answers auto-save every 15 seconds
- After submission, see your score and review answers

## 9. Chat

> **Go to:** Sidebar → Communication Center

### Send a Message

1. Click **Communication Center**.
2. Click **+** or **New Chat**.

## 13. Enrollment

> **Go to:** Portal website → Enrollment (no login needed)

### Apply for Enrollment

1. Go to the **Enrollment** page.
2. Select **Enrollment Type** (New Student, Returning, Transferee, SHS, Parent-Assisted).
3. Fill in **Personal Info** (name, sex, DOB, birthplace, nationality).
4. Fill in **Address** (street, barangay, city, province, zip).
5. Fill in **Parent/Guardian Info**.
6. Fill in **Academic Info** (grade level, LRN, previous school).
7. **Upload Documents** (birth cert, report card, ID picture — PDF/JPEG/PNG, max 5MB each).
8. **Review** everything.
9. Click **Submit Application**.

### After Submitting

- Save your **reference number** (e.g., ENR-2025-XXXX)
- Use it to track your application

**Tip:** Your form auto-saves. You can close and come back later.

## 14. Track Enrollment

> **Go to:** Portal website → Track Enrollment (no login needed)

1. Enter your **reference number**.
2. Enter your **email**.
3. Click **Track**.

### Status Stages

**Pending** → **Under Review** → **Approved** → **Enrolled**

- **Pending Requirements** — you need to upload more docs
- **Rejected** — check remarks for reason

## 15. FAQ

**Can't log in?**
- Check your LRN (12 digits) and password
- Caps Lock off?
- Locked out? Wait 15 minutes
- Contact admin

**Forgot password?**
- Click "Forgot Password?" → enter email → follow link
- Or visit school office

**Quiz timed out?**
- Your answers are auto-submitted and graded
- Check if you have remaining attempts

**Can't upload documents?**
- Max 5MB per file
- PDF, JPEG, or PNG only
- Try a different browser

**Don't see grades?**
- Teacher may not have posted yet
- Check Grades tab in My Classes
- Ask your teacher

**Page broken on phone?**
- Refresh (Ctrl+F5)
- Update browser
- Clear cache
